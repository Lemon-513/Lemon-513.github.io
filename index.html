Biography

I study data science at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. My main research interest is information diffusion: how the structure of a social network shapes who receives a message and how far it travels.

I also work with operational data. In a study of sorting efficiency, I brought separate records together and used visualisation and regression to examine differences in performance.

Research interests

Information diffusion

I use network measures and diffusion models to study how the connections between people shape the spread of a message.

Selected work

Analysing sorting efficiency

I cleaned and joined operational records, used plots to compare sorting performance, and fitted regression models to examine which factors were associated with efficiency.

Modelling information spread

I explored how users and their connections contribute to the spread of information, using network measures to describe the structure and diffusion models to study how a message travels through it.
